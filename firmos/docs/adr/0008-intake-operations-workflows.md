# ADR-0008: Intake operations workflows and Gusto hours handoff

**Status:** Approved and implemented (2026-11-13)

## Context

The full 2026-11-13 Yecny × Agentic transcript calls for one selector behavior across intake, inline qualifying questions, reusable vendor and report knowledge, client-specific pricing and schedules, accurate existing-client conversion, and a Gusto path that does not recreate payroll. Earlier local option lists could not distinguish shared definitions from client-only work, and the client record had no payroll handoff.

## Decision

1. Keep related answers on the question that qualifies them. CPA details, equity choices, and custom closing deadlines remain inline during the initial walk and review edits. `INTAKE_GROUP_CHILDREN` declares progressive vertical groups with one navigation row and unchanged answer keys for Real estate, LLC classification, referral details, merchant processors, deposits, payroll, bill payment, and accounting software. Category and qualifying-card navigation establishes a new current position; links to a grouped child resolve to its parent, and Previous and Continue always traverse adjacent visible cards rather than returning to a jump origin.
2. Store closing deadlines as numeric 1–365 day offsets. Keep the legacy 5/10/15 pricing tier separately, and add the actual number of calendar days to the period end rather than clamping to a day-of-month value. This supports longer cases such as October work due 45 days later on December 15.
3. Store reusable vendor and report metadata on `option_list_values`. Vendor metadata supports a client scope; `client_vendor_assignments` persists selected cadence, payment method, portal override, and SOP link. Report scope and shared SOP belong to the definition; cadence and price belong to the client.
4. Convert vendor and specialty-report selections into recurring routines on both scheduler and legacy conversion paths. Reuse shared SOP links rather than copying procedure text. Portal links must use HTTPS without embedded credentials or credential query parameters.
5. Existing-client conversion preserves original start dates and historical records without generating retroactive projects, quote lines, or catch-up task runs. Persist skipped next-run dates.
6. Use the single-select 1099 choice for no work, collection, or full management, with an optional inline filing estimate. Pricing specialty reports uses either a flat price per report or estimated hours.
7. Show a Gusto hours handoff on the client Overview only when its payroll provider names Gusto. A staff-only route exports one row per employee per client-worked day. The Payroll report separately offers a firm-wide worksheet through an owner/admin-only route, including general day-clock time. Union overlapping day, activity, and task timers and subtract unpaid breaks; never sum stored duration fields. Include full-week tracked work when estimating overtime for a midweek period.
8. The CSV is a review worksheet, not a guaranteed import. Gusto's documented Smart Import can accept spreadsheet/CSV hours, but staff must review its field/employee/pay-period mapping before payroll. A 40-hour Monday–Sunday threshold estimates overtime across all tracked work, proportionally allocated to worked days. FirmOS does not infer employee eligibility, daily/state overtime, double overtime, or PTO. Gusto opens in a new tab; no iframe, credential collection, OAuth tokens, live payroll updates, or submission are included.
9. Closeout remediation adds Cash as a validated vendor-payment method through selection, conversion, and recurring-work labels. Monthly and twice-monthly period boundaries and pay dates are optional; incomplete mappings are labeled Pay schedule to confirm. Provider, frequency, and who handles payroll remain required, existing answer keys stay unchanged, and an omitted twice-monthly pay date retains the close-cadence scheduling fallback. The custom close option describes its actual 1–365 day range.
10. The 2026-11-18 workflow adds End of month as stored value 0 on payroll period ends and pay dates, never period starts. The routine schedule retains 0 for display and maps it to day 31 only when constructing the existing domain rule, whose month-length engine handles February and leap years. Business income deposited into personal accounts receives its own required intake question, inline explanation, review summary, and recurring review. All four commingling reviews follow close cadence.
11. Vendor selection uses a multi-select of vendors with existing SOPs plus a single-search all-vendor picker that adds to the first. Industry-used vendors rank first, then alphabetical; missing or unknown industries use alphabetical order. New catalog definitions are shared by default, while legacy client-only scope remains respected. Vendor cadence, portal overrides, record/pay routines, and report pricing remain client-specific.
12. Every SOP-linked vendor and report field records `existing`, `create`, or `unsure` separately from the SOP ID. `client_vendor_assignments.sop_status` persists the vendor choice. Create/unsure derives one idempotent current follow-up task plus a needs-SOP marker, never an empty SOP template. Existing links remain shared without copying procedure content.

## Consequences

- Stable catalog definitions survive frequency and pricing differences between clients; one-off entries stay scoped to the originating intake.
- Existing accounts and answers remain backward compatible, including legacy vendor lists, equity choices, pay-date fields, and report definitions.
- Staff must review employee/pay-period mapping and overtime before entering hours in Gusto. An empty client-scoped CSV means no client-attributed activity or task timers in the period, not that staff worked no hours. The owner/admin firm-wide export includes general day-clock time and is the appropriate complete-hours review worksheet.
- Live Gusto integration remains a separate, approval-gated decision. Embedded production access, company onboarding, payroll calculation, and submission were not built.
